Single-Person Generation

Stable Identity Across Motion, Pose and Viewpoint

Vera focuses on fine-grained facial identity rather than only coarse subject appearance, aiming to preserve a recognizable person under expression changes, head movement, walking, talking, object interaction and changing viewpoints.
